SayDo website
=============

See it on your computer
  Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser. No install is needed.

Put it on the internet (GitHub Pages, free)
  1. Go to github.com and sign in.
  2. Click the + at the top right, then "New repository".
  3. Repository name: saydo    Choose "Public".    Click "Create repository".
  4. On the next page click the link "uploading an existing file".
  5. Open this folder on your computer, select EVERYTHING inside it
     (index.html, style.css, app.js, MODEL_CARD.md, README.txt and the folder "data")
     and drag it all into the browser page. Wait until every file is listed.
  6. Click "Commit changes".
  7. Click "Settings" (top of the repository), then "Pages" in the left menu.
  8. Under "Branch" choose "main", keep "/ (root)", click "Save".
  9. Wait about 2 minutes, refresh the page. Your address appears at the top:
     https://tirthchheta511.github.io/saydo/

What is in this folder
  index.html   the page
  style.css    how it looks
  app.js       how it works
  data/        your results from the Phase 9 notebook (18 files)
  MODEL_CARD.md  the one-page description of the models and their limits
